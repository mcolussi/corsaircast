Corsair Cast

Podcasts, no eldritch horrors.

A podcast player for iPhone and iPad that answers to you, not to advertisers.

Beneath the waterline, in depths where no sane listener should stray, the old podcast apps have swollen into something vast and tentacled: a cyclopean apparatus of conflicting interests, coiled around everything you listen to and quietly forwarding what it finds to an office no one has seen and no petition can reach.

Corsair Cast is a small, fast ship that wants none of it. No account to apply for. No tracking, no file opened in your name. No server of mine where your data waits in some unlit corridor. Your shows, playlists and listening stay on your devices, synced through your own iCloud, and no one will ever summon you to account for them.

Free here, paid elsewhere

  • Uncheck a chapter and it’s skipped

  • Bring your shows over from Apple Podcasts or an OPML file

Premium: ad skipping

Premium splits your downloaded episodes into chapters and gives the ads their own, which are skipped automatically. Up to 150 episodes a month. To do this, Corsair Cast sends the episode’s transcript and metadata to Anthropic’s Claude. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

An auto-renewing subscription, with a free trial for new subscribers. Cancel anytime in Settings at least a day before it renews.

Support

Something broken, something missing, or a show that won’t load? Write to corsaircast@gmail.com, or use Settings → Send Feedback in the app.

  • Moving from another app: Settings → Import & Export brings in Apple Podcasts or an OPML file.
  • Premium on a new device: Restore Purchase on the Premium screen.
  • Cancelling Premium: your Apple Account’s subscriptions, in the Settings app.

Corsair Cast is made by one independent developer. Premium keeps them in coconut rum and orgeat. Welcome to the crew.
